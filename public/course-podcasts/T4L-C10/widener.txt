WHAT WILL BE ASSESSED
The whole segment is short, about seven minutes, and the questions come from the tool
picks and the two buying questions, not the play-by-play of Martínez cutting an onion or
the cast iron cleaning story. Focus on what the reviewers found when they compared their
$40 Victorinox chef's knife to a $300 knife under a microscope at MIT, the surface they
say will instantly dull a knife, and the two questions Hannah Crowley says to ask before
buying any gadget: do I already have this, and will I use it. Keep her air fryer and
convection oven example in mind for those two questions.
